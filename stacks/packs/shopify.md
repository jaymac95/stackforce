# Shopify stack pack

**Checks:** `["shopify theme check"]`

## Conventions
- Customize themes with Liquid sections and blocks; keep changes in the theme repo.
- Use metafields for structured content rather than hard-coding.
- Apps for functionality before custom code.

## Pitfalls
- Don't fork a theme so heavily you can't take updates.
- Keep third-party scripts minimal; they slow the storefront.
- Test checkout changes carefully — they affect real orders.

## Handoff
- Document which app does what and the monthly cost of each.
